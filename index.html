HOW TO PLAY

🎯 The Goal

Play as a native species defending against an invasive threat! Collect your specific food items to survive, refill your ammo, and score points.

🎮 Controls

  • Move: WASD or Arrow Keys (Mobile: Touch Joystick)
  • Shoot: Hold Spacebar (Mobile: Hold FIRE button)

⚠️ Mechanics

  • Hunger: Your hunger constantly drains. If it hits 0%, you starve! Collect food to restore it.
  • Ammo: Shooting costs ammo. Collecting food grants +200 Ammo.
  • Babies: Protect them! If an enemy touches them, you lose ammo and hunger. If they survive, they grow into adults, shoot at enemies, and breed!
  • Power-ups: Grab glowing orbs for powerful temporary abilities.
